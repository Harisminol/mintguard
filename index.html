Check a mint before you connect your wallet.

Paste a mint page link or a contract address. MintGuard screens it for drainer patterns, impersonation, and risky approvals — on your device first, in the cloud only when a deeper look is needed.

Paste at least one of the two. The quick screen runs on-device when Gemini Nano is available; nothing leaves your device for that step.

How the hybrid check works

  1. On-device first. The quick screen runs on Gemini Nano in your browser when a local model is available. It is fast, private, and free — your input never leaves the device for this step.
  2. Cloud when it matters. If no on-device model is available, or when you ask for a deep analysis, the same request goes to Gemini in the cloud through Firebase AI Logic for a fuller verdict and checklist.
  3. You always see where it ran. Every answer carries a badge — On-device · Gemini Nano or Cloud · Gemini — so the routing is never hidden from you.

What MintGuard cannot check

  • It cannot browse the live mint page or read the contract from the chain — it reasons from what you paste. Always verify a contract on a block explorer before minting.
  • A “SAFE” verdict is not a guarantee. Scams evolve; treat every result as a second opinion, not permission to connect your wallet.
  • MintGuard never asks you to connect a wallet, sign anything, or approve a transaction. Any mint page that demands a signature just to view it is itself a red flag.
